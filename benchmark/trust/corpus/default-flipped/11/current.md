# Onboarding to Ashgrove

## Your first workspace

Create one from the sidebar. It is private, so share it deliberately once there
is something to show: add people individually, or raise the visibility from the
workspace settings.

## Finding other people's work

Search covers what you can already see, so a colleague's workspace appears only
after they share it with you. Ask for a link rather than expecting search to
surface it.
