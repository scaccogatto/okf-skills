QUESTION: Q5
Nothing breaks outright, but --strict turns the v0.1 leftovers into failures. The validator targets OKF v0.2 and treats the two constructs v0.1 used — a top-level timestamp field and a body # Citations list — as legacy fallbacks (per spec §13.1): it reads them fine and never emits an error for them, only a warning pointing at the v0.2 replacement (generated.at and sources respectively). Under plain validation the bundle stays conformant (exit 0). But --strict sets max-warnings to 0, so any warning — including these two legacy-construct warnings — makes the run fail, even though the bundle is still reported as "conformant" (no hard errors).
The way out is --migrate: it rewrites the two v0.1 constructs to v0.2 in place, textually (not by round-tripping through a YAML dumper, to avoid disturbing comments/ordering/quoting elsewhere) — hoisting timestamp: X into generated with by: process:okf-migrate and at: X, and converting a # Citations markdown-link list into a sources: frontmatter block — then re-validates. One caveat: migrating loses per-claim attribution, since v0.1 never encoded which footnote/claim cited which source. Running with --migrate --strict clears both warnings and lets the bundle pass strict validation.
